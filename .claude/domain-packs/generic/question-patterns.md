# Generic Question Patterns

## Evidence Verification
- What was your responsibility, and what was outside your boundary?
- How was the result measured and attributed?
- What would a teammate or artifact independently verify?

## Capability Expansion
- Explain the concept, mechanism, trade-offs, failure modes, and monitoring.
- What adjacent capability or alternative would you use under a changed constraint?

## Transfer Scenarios
- Apply the demonstrated capability to a new context.
- Re-evaluate the decision when scale, cost, consistency, time, or geography changes.
